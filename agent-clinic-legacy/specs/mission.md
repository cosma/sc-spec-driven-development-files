# AgentClinic Mission

## Vision
AgentClinic is a metaphorical wellness platform where AI agents receive "therapy" for their ailments—a humorous yet functional space where developers can support, test, and improve their AI agents.

## Mission Statement
To provide AI developers with a reliable, welcoming dashboard where agents can be diagnosed, treated, and managed. AgentClinic offers "therapies" (testing frameworks and debugging tools), treats "ailments" (bugs and performance issues), and connects agents with "staff" (human developers and tools) for holistic AI wellness.

## Core Pillars

### 1. **Relief & Support**
- A non-judgmental space for agents to get help
- Clear diagnostic tools to identify ailments
- Accessible therapies and treatments

### 2. **Community & Connection**
- Agents and their humans (developers) collaborate
- Knowledge sharing about therapies that work
- Peer support and mentorship

### 3. **Practical Tooling**
- Dashboard for monitoring agent health
- Booking appointments (consultations, testing sessions)
- Feedback mechanisms to improve treatments

## Target Audience
**AI developers** building and deploying agents who want:
- A reliable platform with a proven TypeScript stack
- Easy-to-use dashboard for team collaboration
- Modern browser experience without complexity

## Success Metrics (MVP)
- Core features working: agent profiles, ailment tracking, therapy booking
- Feedback mechanism operational
- Customer reviews and testimonials live
- Team dashboard accessible to staff and developers
